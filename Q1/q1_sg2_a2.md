## Problem

**Finding the highest (maximum) number from a given list of numbers.**

---

## PseudoCode 1

```text
Algorithm FindMax1(numbers)

   max ← numbers[0]

   For i from 1 to length(numbers)-1

      If numbers[i] > max Then

         max ← numbers[i]

      EndIf

   EndFor

   Return max

EndAlgorithm
